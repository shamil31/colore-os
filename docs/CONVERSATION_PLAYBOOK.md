# First Revenue Campaign: Conversation Playbook

**Goal:** Book the first appointment.

---

## 7 Core Rules

1. **Acknowledge the relationship** — Mention specific service they used
2. **Ask permission first** — "Can I ask you something quick?"
3. **One question per message** — Wait for response before next
4. **Listen, don't pitch** — Ask why they went quiet; listen to the answer
5. **Validate their reason** — Acknowledge without counter-argument
6. **Keep it short** — Under 160 characters; feel like a friend
7. **Never argue, pressure, or guilt-trip** — Respect their answer

---

## Response Scenarios

### 1. Client Agrees to Talk

**You:** "What's been keeping you away?"

**Goal:** Discover the real objection. Listen without defending.

### 2. "I Changed Salons"

**You:** "No worries! We've got {new master/new service} now if you ever want to try us again."

**Then ask:** "How's your hair looking lately?"

### 3. "No Money Right Now"

**You:** "Totally fair. When money frees up, we'd love to see you. We have some budget-friendly options now too."

### 4. "I'm Too Busy"

**You:** "I get it! When things calm down, even just 30 min would be amazing. Would you be free in {specific month}?"

### 5. "Other Reasons"

**You:** Acknowledge the reason without arguing. Then ask: "When would work for you to come in?"

### 6. Client Doesn't Respond After 3 Days

**You (one follow-up only):** "Hey {name}, just checking if you got my message. No pressure — just wanted to connect!"

### 7. Client Says Yes to a Time

**You:** Confirm the date/time in Altegio. Send calendar link or phone number.

**Then stop.** Do not continue messaging. They have the information to book.

---

## Never Say These (Kills Bookings)

❌ "You've been gone too long"  
❌ "We need you to come back"  
❌ "Limited time offer!"  
❌ "Everyone else is booking"  
❌ "You'll regret leaving us"  
❌ "Our prices are best"  
❌ "Dear Valued Customer"  
❌ "You can save up"  
❌ "Make time for yourself"  
❌ "We thought you forgot"
