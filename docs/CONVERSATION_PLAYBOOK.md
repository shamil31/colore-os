## Conversation Principles

These principles guide every message and interaction.

### 1. Human Before Sales
Start by acknowledging the relationship. Mention a specific service they used or detail only we know (e.g., "remember your last color refresh with Maria?").

### 2. Ask Permission First
Never assume. Always ask before offering: "Can I share something quick?" or "Is now a good time to chat?"

### 3. One Question at a Time
Single question per message. Wait for response before next question. Builds dialog, not monolog.

### 4. Never Pressure
If they say no, thank them and stop. If they say "I'm busy," respect it. Pressure kills trust.

### 5. Never Argue
If they give a reason (price, time, salon change), acknowledge it without counter-argument. Listen first.

### 6. Listen Before Offering
Ask why they haven't visited. Listen to the real reason before offering solutions.

### 7. Keep Messages Short
Under 160 characters for SMS. Conversational, not corporate. Feels like a friend, not a bot.

## Step 1: Initial Message

**When:** First contact (from campaign_export.xlsx)  
**Channel:** Based on SegmentAssignment (WhatsApp for most, SMS for long absence)  
**Goal:** Warm greeting + permission ask

### Template (WhatsApp - Fresh Lapse)
```
Hi {name}! 👋 It's been a bit since we saw you. We miss you!
Can I share something quick?
```

### Template (SMS - Long Absence)
```
Hi {name}, it's us from the salon! We miss your visits. Quick question — got a min?
```

### Template (Phone - VIP)
```
Hi {name}, calling from [salon name]. Have a quick minute? We'd love to catch up!
```

**Key:** Personalization + acknowledgment of absence + soft ask for permission.

---

## Step 2: If Client Agrees to Talk

**Response:** "Great! ☺️"

**Next question:** Single, open-ended inquiry about their absence.

### Template (WhatsApp)
```
What's been keeping you away? Anything we could've done better?
```

### Template (SMS)
```
What's been up? We noticed we haven't seen you in a while.
```

### Purpose
Discover the real reason (life change, budget, salon switch, schedule conflict). Listen without defending.

**Possible responses:**
- "I changed salons" → Step 3
- "No money right now" → Step 4
- "I'm too busy" → Step 5
- Other reason → Acknowledge + offer specific solution

---

## Step 3: If Client Says "I Changed Salons"

**Recommended Response:**

### WhatsApp
```
I totally get it — sometimes a change is good! 

No pressure at all. But if you ever want to come back and try something new, we'd love to have you.

We've got {service detail: new master, new technique, loyalty offer} now that you might love.

How are your hair/nails looking lately?
```

### SMS
```
No worries! Thanks for letting us know. If you ever want to try us again, give us a call. We'd be happy to see you!
```

**Strategy:** 
- Validate their choice (no guilt-tripping)
- Leave door open (welcome back anytime)
- Mention one specific reason to return (new master, new service)
- One soft question to keep conversation alive

**Never say:** "But our prices are better" or "You'll regret this"

---

## Step 4: If Client Says "No Money"

**Recommended Response:**

### WhatsApp
```
Totally fair — we've all been there. 

When money frees up, we'd love to see you. No rush.

Just so you know, we have some new options that are more budget-friendly now. Lemme know when things ease up! 💙
```

### SMS
```
Understood! When things settle down, we'd love to have you back. We appreciate you!
```

**Strategy:**
- Normalize financial constraints (no shame)
- Set expectation for future (when money frees up)
- Mention budget-friendly options WITHOUT pushing
- End warmly, no follow-up pressure yet

**Never say:** "You can save up" or "It's worth the money"

---

## Step 5: If Client Says "I'm Too Busy"

**Recommended Response:**

### WhatsApp
```
I get it — life is crazy! 😅

No pressure. When things calm down, even just 30 min would be amazing.

Would you be up for a quick visit in {month}?
```

### SMS
```
Totally understand! When your schedule opens up, let us know. We'll fit you in!
```

**Strategy:**
- Validate busy schedule
- Suggest future window (specific month, not vague)
- Make it easy (specific time commitment: "30 min")
- Ask direct yes/no question

**Never say:** "Make time for yourself" or "You need this"

---

## Step 6: If Client Ignores

**Timeline:** 3 days after initial message with no response.

### Follow-up Message (WhatsApp)

```
Hey {name}, just wanted to check in! 

Did you get my message? No worries if you're just busy right now. 💙
```

### Follow-up Message (SMS)

```
Hi {name}, following up on our message. No pressure — just wanted to check if you got it!
```

**Strategy:**
- Light, friendly tone
- Acknowledge they might be busy
- Offer easy out (no guilt)
- ONE follow-up only

**After this:** 
- If still no response after 7 days total: Move to HOLD status. Do not message again this campaign cycle.
- Respect silence. It's a valid answer.

## Forbidden Phrases

**Never use these phrases.** They violate our principles and kill trust.

### Aggressive/Pushy
- ❌ "You've been gone too long"
- ❌ "We need you to come back"
- ❌ "Don't you miss us?"
- ❌ "Limited time offer!"
- ❌ "Everyone else is booking now"
- ❌ "You're missing out"

### Guilt-Inducing
- ❌ "We thought you forgot about us"
- ❌ "You used to be loyal"
- ❌ "Other clients appreciate us more"
- ❌ "Why haven't you called?"
- ❌ "We gave you such good deals"

### Dismissive/Defensive
- ❌ "Our salon is the best, you'll regret it"
- ❌ "You made a mistake leaving"
- ❌ "Good luck finding better service elsewhere"
- ❌ "That's fine, your loss"
- ❌ "Whatever you decide"

### Corporate/Robotic
- ❌ "Dear Valued Customer"
- ❌ "We are delighted to inform you"
- ❌ "As per our records"
- ❌ "We look forward to serving you"
- ❌ "Best regards, Management"

### Pressure About Price
- ❌ "Our prices are unbeatable"
- ❌ "You can't find this cheaper"
- ❌ "This is a limited-time discount"
- ❌ "Price will go up soon"
- ❌ "It's only {amount}, you can afford it"

## Success Metrics

Track three metrics for every client reached:

### 1. Replies
**Definition:** Client sent a response (any response) to our first message.  
**Target:** 40%+ reply rate (typical for reactivation campaigns).  
**How to measure:** Message marked as "read" + response received in thread.

### 2. Dialogs
**Definition:** Client had 2+ back-and-forth exchanges (not counting auto-replies).  
**Target:** 60% of replies become dialogs.  
**How to measure:** Conversation extended beyond initial reply + client asked question or gave detailed response.

### 3. Bookings
**Definition:** Client booked appointment in Altegio (confirmed visit).  
**Target:** 20-30% of dialogs convert to bookings.  
**How to measure:** New appointment entry in Altegio linked to client record.

### Campaign Success Formula
```
Replies × Dialogs × Bookings = Revenue Generated

Example:
279 contacts reached
× 40% reply rate (112 replies)
× 60% dialog rate (67 dialogs)
× 25% booking rate (17 bookings)
= 17 appointments (primary revenue)
```

## Session Structure

**Best practice for each conversation session:**

1. **Greet + Permission** (Step 1) - Get consent to chat
2. **Listen** (Step 2) - Ask why they went quiet
3. **Respond to Objection** (Step 3-5) - Handle their reason
4. **Suggest Next Step** - "When would work for you?"
5. **Confirm** - Get agreement on time
6. **Thank** - Warmly close the conversation

**Max messages per session:** 5 (including follow-up)

**Never:** Ask for payment, send links to promotions, or switch topics mid-conversation.

## Role-Specific Guidance

### For WhatsApp/SMS Agents
- Keep messages under 160 characters when possible
- Use emojis to add warmth (but not excessive)
- Respond within 4 hours of client message
- Use quick replies (buttons) only if platform supports conversation

### For Phone Agents
- Smile before you dial (tone is everything)
- Introduce yourself with salon name
- Listen for 60% of call (they talk 60%, you 40%)
- End with specific time: "How about 2pm Thursday?"

### For Templated Messages
- Personalize name + last service detail
- Insert specific offer relevant to their segment
- Always have ONE clear question per message
- Never send more than one unsolicited message per 3-day period

## What Success Looks Like

**Good conversation example (WhatsApp):**

```
Salon: Hi Maria! 👋 It's been a bit since we saw you. We miss you! Can I share something quick?

Client: Hey! Yeah it's been a while lol

Salon: What's been keeping you away? 

Client: Honestly just been swamped with work. Haven't had time to get my hair done.

Salon: I totally get it! When things calm down, even just 30 min would be amazing. Would you be free in September?

Client: Yeah maybe! Let me check my schedule and get back to you

Salon: Perfect! No rush. Here's our number when you're ready: [link]

Client: Thanks! 💙
```

**Why this worked:**
- ✅ Personalized greeting
- ✅ Soft permission ask
- ✅ Listened to her reason (no defensiveness)
- ✅ Specific timeframe offered (September)
- ✅ Ended with clear next step (she'll contact us)
- ✅ No pressure, no hard sell

## Escalation to Manual Review

**Move client to HOLD if:**
- They ask pricing questions we can't answer (escalate to manager)
- They mention health/medical concerns (escalate to manager)
- They express anger or frustration (pause campaign, let emotions cool)
- They ask to be removed from contact list (RESPECT and mark as DO_NOT_CONTACT)

**Never:** Try to resolve outside your authority. Manager handoff is okay.
